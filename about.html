Athena Saghi Projects About

Athena (Zeinabsadat) Saghi is a Human-Centered AI Researcher working at the intersection of cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). She is a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, in the Adaptive Computing (ACE) Lab, advised by Prof. Souti Chattopadhyay.

She works across neuroscience and computing, pairing behavioral studies with physiological sensing to understand how AI reshapes human thinking. Her research examines what happens to people’s judgment, creativity and biases when they work with large language models. She also builds human-in-the-loop and agentic systems that sense a person’s cognitive state, from think-aloud speech to EEG, and adapt to it in real time.

She received her M.S. in Computer Science from USC in 2025. Before USC, she graduated from Sharif University of Technology with a bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering. There she was a research assistant in the Data Science and Machine Learning Lab, working on deep learning for EEG and visual attention. She was also a graduate research assistant in the Motion Capture Lab at UC Davis, studying pedagogical agents in virtual reality, and an R&D intern at CommaMed, building deep learning models for medical imaging.
