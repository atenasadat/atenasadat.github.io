Zeinabsadat (Athena) Saghi

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Souti Chattopadhyay in the Adaptive Computing (ACE) Lab.

My work sits between neuroscience, cognitive science and HCI. I study how people work with AI, how AI changes the way they think, and how to build AI that adapts to the person using it.

InterestsHuman-centered AI, physiological sensing (EEG), affective computing, human-in-the-loop and agentic AI, computational cognitive modeling, evaluation of human–AI interaction.
